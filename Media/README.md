# Demonstration provenance

ShadowLab_Demo.mp4 is a silent 1280×720 edit of the author's macOS recording.

The four opening mode views are held frames from source times approximately 2, 6, 8 and 12 seconds. The parameter sections use source 12–28 seconds (emitter size) and 28–48 seconds (sphere height). The animation section uses 76–88 seconds. Moving sections retain recorded speed. The scene is cropped and enlarged; title and footer overlays are editorial labels.

The final card reports the separate standalone Player benchmark documented in Docs/RESULTS.md. It is not timing extracted from the recording. The depth-preview section was omitted because its preview was outside the recorded region.

preview.jpg and comparison.jpg are derived from the same footage; no scene imagery was synthesized.


The homepage preview `sphere-height.gif` uses only 26–46 seconds of the edited MP4 (the sphere-height section), at 800×450 and 15 fps, looping automatically. The full MP4 is retained.
